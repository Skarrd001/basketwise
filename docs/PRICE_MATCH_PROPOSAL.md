# BasketWise Price Match: proposal for Pick n Pay

## Win the shoppers who almost chose you

BasketWise shows shoppers which store is cheapest for their **whole basket**, including the cost of
driving there or delivery. On our 15-item standard basket (23 November 2026, identical products matched
by barcode) **Pick n Pay is the cheapest store for shoppers without loyalty cards**, but **6.8% more
expensive than Checkers for card-holders** (R640,90 vs R599,90). Those card-holders are deciding where
to shop right now, and Pick n Pay is close enough to win them.

## How it works

1. A shopper builds a basket in BasketWise. **The ranking stays honest**: if Checkers is cheapest, it
   shows first.
2. If Pick n Pay is within a gap **you** set (for example 7%), a clearly labelled box appears:
   *"Sponsored offer: Pick n Pay will match R599,90 on this basket."*
3. The shopper taps it and gets a **single-use code**, valid for 48 hours and tied to their basket.
4. They shop at Pick n Pay and redeem the code at the till or on asap!. **You pay BasketWise only for
   redeemed baskets.**

## Commercials

- **No upfront cost.** A fee of **R[__] per redeemed basket** (or **[__]% of basket value**), invoiced monthly.
- **You control the exposure:** the maximum gap (%), the eligible categories (e.g. staples only; no
  alcohol or tobacco), a cap per shopper per week, and a monthly budget.
- **Why it beats a leaflet:** you pay only for shoppers who were about to shop elsewhere, and they
  arrive with a whole basket, not a single discounted item.

## Safeguards

- **Neutral ranking, labelled offer.** The comparison is never changed or reordered for payment, and
  the offer is always marked *Sponsored* (Consumer Protection Act s41; ARB Code).
- **Prices you can trust.** Products are matched by barcode; a match is offered only on prices less
  than 48 hours old, each with its date. With an official Pick n Pay feed, the match uses your own prices.
- **Fraud controls.** Single-use codes, 48-hour expiry, linked to the Smart Shopper card where possible,
  with weekly caps.
- **No personal data shared.** You receive redemption counts and basket totals per code, not
  shoppers' names or contact details (POPIA).

## Pilot proposal

Three months, one region (e.g. Western Cape), staples only, a capped monthly budget. Measured on
redemptions, average basket value, and how many redeemers had previously shopped at a competitor.

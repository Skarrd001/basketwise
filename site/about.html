How BasketWise works

Prices

Every morning an automated job reads the current price of a core basket of everyday products from each retailer's public website. It follows each retailer's robots.txt, identifies itself honestly and makes only a few hundred requests a day in total. Products are matched across retailers by barcode, so you're comparing the identical item.

Coverage, honestly

  • Checkers, Shoprite: online prices, which Checkers states match in-store. Xtra Savings card deals are shown separately.
  • Pick n Pay: online prices for PnP's default pricing region. Your store may differ.
  • SPAR: each SPAR is independently owned and sets its own prices. Only published SPAR specials are available.
  • Woolworths: not included. Woolworths blocks automated access, and we respect that.

Travel cost

We find the nearest store of each chain (store locations from OpenStreetMap), estimate road distance as straight-line distance × 1.3, there and back, and multiply by your car's fuel use and the petrol price. You can also give your time a value. For two-store "split shops" we estimate home → store A → store B → home.

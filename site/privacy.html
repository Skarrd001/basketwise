The short version

Your location and your basket stay in your browser. We have no accounts, no advertising trackers and no analytics.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", your browser gives your position to the BasketWise page running on your device. The distance and cost calculations happen on your device, and your location is not sent to our servers. If you type a suburb, that text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to find it on the map (their privacy policy). Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which see your IP address and the map area you view.

Stored on your device

Your basket, location and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. Clear your browser data to remove them.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you (we hold none) and complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

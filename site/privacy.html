The short version

You can use BasketWise fully without giving us anything. Your location is never sent to us. If you choose to sign in or subscribe, we keep only your email address and what you save (your basket and price alerts), use it only for those features, never sell or share it for marketing, and delete it when you ask. We have no advertising trackers and no analytics.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", your browser gives your position to the BasketWise page running on your device. The distance and cost calculations happen on your device, and your location is not sent to our servers or stored in your account. If you type a suburb, that text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to find it on the map (their privacy policy). Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which see your IP address and the map area you view.

Stored on your device

Your basket, location and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there next time. Clear your browser data to remove them.

If you sign in (optional)

What we collect: your email address; the basket you save (product codes and quantities); your price-drop alerts (product codes and the price when you set each alert); and the date and time these were last changed. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address and name; we don't receive your Google password or contacts.

Why: to show you your saved basket on any device and to email you when a product you chose gets cheaper. That is the only use.

No password is stored: you sign in with a one-time link sent to your email (or with Google).

How long: until you delete your account. "Delete my account" (under My account) immediately deletes your account, saved basket and alerts.

Weekly prices email (optional)

If you subscribe, we keep your email address, the wording you agreed to and when, and whether you confirmed. We send you the weekly update only after you confirm by clicking the link in our first email (POPIA section 69 consent). Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, which deletes your address from the list. You can subscribe without signing in.

Who processes your information

Account and subscriber data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and emails are sent through [email provider, e.g. Brevo]. Their servers may be outside South Africa; we use them under agreements that require them to protect your information to a standard comparable to POPIA (section 72). We do not sell, rent or share your information with retailers, advertisers or anyone else.

Security

Access to account data is restricted by the database so that each user can read and change only their own basket and alerts. The subscriber list can't be read from the website at all. If a security breach affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its use. Email [e-mail]. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
